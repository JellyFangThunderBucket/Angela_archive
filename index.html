The woman under the cape

The ridiculous part is fictional. The part underneath it is not.

Angela started in emergency medicine at Danville Regional Medical Center and continued at Morehead Memorial Hospital, now UNC Health Rockingham. Over time she became the kind of person who ends up carrying charge-nurse responsibility, care coordination, scheduling, leadership, and the unspoken assignment of knowing what to do when everybody else is out of ideas.

She has raised four children, kept learning, kept leading, survived hard chapters, and somehow kept showing up. The superhero story exists because plain language started feeling inadequate.

Selected incident reports

Documented acts of completely unnecessary heroism

MARITIME / INCIDENT 001

Sinking cruise liner returned to service after Angela simply held the entire thing above the Atlantic

A passenger vessel taking on water sent out a distress call. Before rescue crews could finish coordinating, Angela arrived, put one hand under the hull, and raised approximately 142,000 tons of ship clear of the water while checking the nursing schedule on her phone.

“Ma’am, the ship is sinking.”
“Not anymore.”

All passengers survived. The captain attempted to thank her. Angela asked why the department was understaffed.

AVIATION / 002

Commercial jet caught after dual engine failure

Witnesses report she set it down gently, asked whether anyone was hurt, then immediately wanted to know if the event had to be charted.

SPACE / 003

Extinction-level meteor turns around after receiving one-word response

NASA sent coordinates. Angela replied, “No.” Deep-space tracking recorded what astronomers called an extremely respectful U-turn.

WEATHER / 004

Category 5 hurricane redirected after being asked whether it was serious right now

The storm weakened, moved offshore, and reportedly spent several hours considering its choices.

INFRASTRUCTURE / 005

Bridge pauses mid-collapse

Angela told it to “hold on a damn minute.” It did. Engineers have added a provisional load category titled Angela said wait.

WILDLIFE / 006

42-foot raccoon leaves county after brief conversation

She pointed toward the tree line and said, “Go.” It was later located three states away making better choices.
